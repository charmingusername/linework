Linework

Direct MP3 Audio & CSV Fabrication Suite

📖 Information & Controls

Linework translates site-specific sound recordings (speeches, atmospheric audio, music) or 3D point data (CSV) into fabricable 3D linear sculptures formatted as continuous pipe runs or timber profiles.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • O: Switch to Orbit Mode (Left-click & drag to rotate view).
  • H: Switch to Pan Mode (Left-click & drag to slide/pan view).

Acoustic Analysis

The program analyzes 1,200 acoustic frames across two key metrics:

  • RMS Energy (Volume): Drives macro loop expansion, swoop depth, and spatial volume.
  • Zero Crossing Rate (Frequency/Texture): Modulates helical frequencies, axial twisting, and cursive looping density.

Form Tuning & Smoothing

  • Coiling Frequency: Defaults to 1.5x for balanced, self-contained cursive looping right out of the box.
  • Extended Spatial Smoothing: Adjust Gaussian smoothing up to 30 passes to eliminate micro-kinks across larger or higher-frequency forms.
  • Dynamic Self-Avoidance: Automatically maintains clearance based on outer material diameter while allowing tangential touching.
  • Kink Warning: Highlights sections in amber where centerline curvature exceeds material bend limits.
